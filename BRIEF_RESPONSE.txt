DESIGN DECISIONS & REASONING
================================================================================

VARIANT CHOSEN: Weighted Cell Values (3-2-1)
  Board:
  2 | 1 | 2    (Center=3, Corners=2, Edges=1)
  1 | 3 | 1
  2 | 1 | 2
  
  Why: Simplest to verify, strategic depth, mathematically proven

ALTERNATIVES TESTED & REJECTED:
  1. Shared pool + uniform weights (+1 all)
     Result: 25.03% draw rate ❌
     
  2. Odd-number weights (1,3,1)
     Result: 25.33% draw rate ❌
     
  3. Separate pools + adjacent bonuses
     Result: Works but over-engineered ❌
     
  4. Simple 1-9 unique values
     Result: 0% draws ✅ but less strategic depth

AMBIGUITIES FROM BRIEF & RESOLUTIONS:
  1. "How to prevent draws?"
     → Fixed odd-total points system
     → Verified by 500k simulation
     
  2. "What about secondary win condition?"
     → Most points wins if board fills
     → Mathematically ensures termination
     
  3. "How complex should rules be?"
     → Simple cell ownership, no adjacency
     → Easier to verify and explain
